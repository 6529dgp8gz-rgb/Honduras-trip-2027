💡 Flight Search Tips
🕐
Best booking window
Book now — you're in the ideal 3–6 month window for December. Prices rise sharply after August.
🛫
Best departure airports
Frankfurt (FRA) has most competition and usually cheapest fares. Berlin (BER) and Prague (PRG) worth checking. London works well if you can get a cheap positioning flight.
📅
Avoid these dates
Dec 21–26 are the most expensive days of the year on transatlantic routes. Target Dec 1–10 or Dec 15–19 for better prices.
📆
Best days to fly
Fly Tuesday–Thursday. Thursday departures average 16% cheaper than Sundays. Avoid Friday and Sunday departures.
🔍
Search strategy
Use Google Flights date grid to compare cheapest days. Set price alerts on Google Flights + Skyscanner. Book outbound and return as separate one-ways for open-jaw routing (fly in CUN, fly out PTY).
✈️
Best airlines for outbound
Condor FRA→CUN direct (often cheapest from Germany). Iberia via Madrid. KLM via Amsterdam. Air France via Paris. Virgin Atlantic via London.
🔄
Return from Panama City (PTY)
Iberia PTY→MAD→FRA/BER. KLM PTY→AMS→BER. Copa Airlines connects to many European hubs via their Panama City hub.
💳
Price drop protection
Book main cabin or higher — if price drops you can rebook for a travel credit. Use a credit card with built-in travel insurance for extra protection.

⚠️ CA-4 Strategy for Your Trip

You enter the CA-4 zone in Guatemala and exit when you fly to Panama. With ~12 days in Guatemala and ~57 days in Honduras, you'll use approximately 69 days of your 90-day allowance — leaving a buffer of ~21 days. This is comfortable as long as you don't extend stays significantly. Flying from Roatán to Panama City fully exits the CA-4 zone and resets everything.

If you run over: Cross into Belize for 1 night (e.g. from Flores or La Ceiba area) — Belize is outside CA-4 and resets your counter upon re-entry.
