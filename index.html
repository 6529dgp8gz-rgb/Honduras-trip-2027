⚠️ CA-4 Strategy for Your Trip

You enter the CA-4 zone in Guatemala and exit when you fly to Panama. With ~12 days in Guatemala and ~57 days in Honduras, you'll use approximately 69 days of your 90-day allowance — leaving a buffer of ~21 days. This is comfortable as long as you don't extend stays significantly. Flying from Roatán to Panama City fully exits the CA-4 zone and resets everything.

If you run over: Cross into Belize for 1 night (e.g. from Flores or La Ceiba area) — Belize is outside CA-4 and resets your counter upon re-entry.
